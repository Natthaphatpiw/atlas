# Atlast Product Overview

## Purpose
Atlast is a standalone frontend application designed to validate customer demand for a device pawn / secured-loan service in Thailand.

## MVP product goal
The MVP helps a seller:
1. Start a valuation request.
2. Choose a device, model, and specification.
3. Answer question-based condition assessment.
4. Enter the expected selling price.
5. View an Atlast estimated valuation.
6. If the seller wants to proceed, provide minimum contact information.
7. Continue to the Atlast LINE Official Account handoff.

## Scope
In scope for the MVP:
- landing page
- valuation funnel
- device selection flow
- condition assessment
- expected price entry
- demo valuation result
- lead capture
- LINE handoff boundary

Out of scope for the MVP:
- investor matching
- contract management
- payments
- renewal interest
- redemption
- logistics
- warehouse management
- staff or inspection system
- Astly integration
- production database or backend
- Supabase integration

## Market
- Thailand
- Currency: THB
- Business objective: validate willingness to sell or pawn devices through the Atlast flow

## Product principles
- Frontend-first for MVP
- Domain logic separated from UI
- Service boundaries explicit
- Mock services for demo and development only
- Future backend adapters isolated and ready
- Astly integration remains future-only and out of scope
- LINE integration placeholder only, not implemented yet
